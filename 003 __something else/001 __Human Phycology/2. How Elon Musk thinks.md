
1. **First-principles thinking (from physics)**  
    Musk says his main “framework for thinking” comes from physics: start from basic truths and reason up, instead of copying what others do.[](https://fs.blog/elon-musk-framework-thinking/)[](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NV3sBlRgzTI)  
    Example: Instead of accepting “rockets are expensive,” he asked: what materials are in a rocket, what do those raw materials cost, and is there a smarter way to arrange them? That led to much cheaper rockets at SpaceX.
    
2. **Challenge all assumptions**  
    He tries not to accept rules just because “that’s how it’s done” or “the legal department says so.” He wants to know _who_ made a requirement and then asks, “Is this actually necessary?”[](https://fs.blog/elon-musk-the-algorithm/)  
    He even says requirements from smart people are the most dangerous because others are afraid to question them.[](https://fs.blog/elon-musk-the-algorithm/)
    
3. **Think long-term (decades, not months)**  
    Many CEOs optimize for the next quarter, but Musk focuses on “where the world will be in 10–20 years” and positions his companies for that future.[](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4wY8Ov0dHRI)  
    That’s why he works on electric cars, rockets, and energy storage—things that matter on a long time horizon.
    
4. **Engineering and details first**  
    Musk dives deeply into technical details instead of only thinking like a “big picture” manager.[](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4wY8Ov0dHRI)  
    This lets him see which constraints are real (physics, safety) and which are just bad design or habit.

## How Elon Musk solves problems (“The Algorithm”)
A well-known summary of his problem-solving pattern (what he calls “the algorithm”) is:[](https://fs.blog/elon-musk-the-algorithm/)

1. **Question every requirement**
    - Find the real person behind each rule.
    - Ask: “Is this actually needed?”
    - Make the requirement “less dumb.”[](https://fs.blog/elon-musk-the-algorithm/)
        
2. **Delete things first**
    - Remove any part or process you can.
    - If you did not have to add back at least 10%, you probably did not delete enough.[](https://fs.blog/elon-musk-the-algorithm/)
        
3. **Simplify and optimize (only after deletion)**
    - Now make what remains simpler and more efficient.
    - Avoid the mistake of optimizing something that should not exist.[](https://fs.blog/elon-musk-the-algorithm/)
        
4. **Accelerate cycle time**
    - Speed up how fast you build and test.
    - Do this only _after_ questioning, deleting, and simplifying.[](https://fs.blog/elon-musk-the-algorithm/)
        
5. **Automate last**
    - Only then use machines or code to automate.
    - Musk admits he made a mistake by trying to automate too early in Tesla’s factories.[](https://fs.blog/elon-musk-the-algorithm/)
